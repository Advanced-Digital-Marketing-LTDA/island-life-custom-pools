Never built a pool before? You don't need to know how it works. We walk you through every step and answer every question along the way.

## How a build runs

1. **Free design consult.** Tell us what you want and what you must have.
2. **3D design.** We model your pool and outdoor space so you can see it before we build.
3. **Excavation.** The crew arrives and the yard becomes a pool.
4. **Structure.** Pool, spa, patio, fireplace: the build takes shape.
5. **Start-up.** The pool is started up and ready to swim.

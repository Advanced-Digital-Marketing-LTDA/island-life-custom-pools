# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static) with TinaCMS for editable content. Custom code, no WordPress/Elementor.

## Users

Houston-area homeowners (Tomball, Cypress, The Woodlands, Houston) researching a pool builder on a phone or laptop. Two audiences with equal weight: first-pool buyers who need to be walked through the whole process, and backyard upgraders adding a pool, patio cover, outdoor kitchen, or pickleball court to a yard they already use.

## Product Purpose

Island Life Custom Pools designs and builds custom pools, patios, and outdoor kitchens. The site exists to get a homeowner to request a free quote or call. Both count equally as success.

## Positioning

The company models the pool and outdoor space in 3D so the homeowner sees the design before it is built. Owner-led (Tim), Google Guaranteed, with communication clients repeatedly name in reviews.

## Operating Context

Free design consult, then 3D design, excavation, structure, start-up. Hours Mon–Fri 8am–7pm, Sat 8am–12pm, Sunday closed. Phones: (832) 304-4092 and (281) 305-8711, both active. Discount for military, first responders, and teachers. Financing through partners. Hayward equipment page, gallery, blog, financing, about, contact pages exist on the current site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Quote form fields: name, email, phone, preferred contact method (email or phone), zip code, interest (pool, patio, outdoor kitchen), message. Current site: WordPress + NitroPack, built by Kreative Media. Both phone numbers are active ((832) 304-4092 in the site footer, (281) 305-8711 in Instagram captions and on the patios page). Which one to show where is undecided; the redesign shows (832) 304-4092 everywhere for now. The quote form endpoint and the deployment target are undecided.

## Brand Commitments

Keep: name "Island Life Custom Pools", logo, "Bring Your Vacation Dreams Home!", "Fast Service. Fair Pricing.", "Experience the Island Life", island/vacation voice, existing nav and page structure. The logo file is `public/images/island-life-logo.svg` (white background: the header blends it with multiply, the footer sets it on a white plate). Brand colors are sampled from it: navy #010031, blue #0c75b6, sky #75b5d2, glow #fdeeb4. The action yellow is the glow hue deepened for contrast.

## Evidence on Hand

Five real client reviews on the homepage (Sam M., Jasmine H., Annette, Dee G., Jeff L.). Google Guaranteed status. A 3D design software video. Eight Instagram posts (patio covers, pickleball, work in progress, night swim, Lake Conroe lakefront, Houston Galleria commercial project). No project photos exist in the project yet. The client will supply them later, so the design stays illustration-led until then and keeps photo slots (Recent work, Photo field in TinaCMS). Do not fabricate photography, generated pool imagery, ratings, review counts, prices, or timelines.

## Product Principles

- Show the design process, not stock photography: the homeowner should see their backyard before it exists.
- Phone and quote form get equal prominence everywhere a decision is asked for.
- Serve first-pool buyers and upgraders with the same page, no audience split in the nav.
- Only claim what the current site or its reviews already say.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target; mobile-first (homeowners browse on phones); respect reduced motion.

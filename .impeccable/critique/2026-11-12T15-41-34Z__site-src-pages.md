## Design Specificity Verdict

Mostly category-interchangeable. The imagery is the one specific asset (labelled 3D renders, palms, torches, no faked photography). Structure and type are the standard pool-builder template; swap the logo and any Texas or Florida builder fits. "Vacation dreams" is a tagline the design never makes you feel. The 3D-design premise, the real differentiator, sits mid-page as a small card.

Deterministic scan: detect.mjs returned zero findings on src, dist and styles (sanity-checked against a known-bad file). The detector cannot see the interchangeability, the inconsistent CTA colour or the duplicated copy.

## Priority Issues

- [P1] The quote action has three colours and the hero one is weakest. Sky pill on video in the hero, yellow in header and bands, near-black in the mobile bar. Fix: one colour (yellow) for every quote action, sky only for neutral links, drop duplicate hero and bar buttons on phones. Command: colorize, polish.
- [P1] Inner pages are the home page re-assembled. Same blocks on every service page, one overview paragraph repeated verbatim in the Design3D block below it, patio and kitchen pages share a paragraph, and the Process band lists excavation on a patio page. Fix: cut the duplicate, give each page its own content from material the site already states, make Process per-page. Command: distill, then clarify.
- [P1] Blog is placeholder and its posts end in a military-discount banner. 1-minute posts, invented dates, same render reused for the pool post, and no next step for a first-pool reader. Fix: publish real posts or hold the blog back; end each post with a context CTA (free design consult plus phone). Command: clarify, layout.
- [P2] Home pacing buries persuasion. Financing at section 5 before Process and reviews, reviews at about 75% scroll, decorative image duo, Instagram text-link list that sends people away before the form. Fix: hero, trust, tiles, 3D film, Process, reviews, Paths, Financing, CTA, form; fold Instagram into the footer. Command: layout, distill.
- [P2] Proof is set small and grey (14px reviews, 13px process text on mid-blue). Fix: 16px body floor, larger quotes, fewer per row. Command: typeset.

## Persona Red Flags

First-time pool buyer on a phone: four call/quote buttons in the first viewport; "Start my pool" and "Plan my upgrade" both scroll to the same form; form fields are bare underlines with a 2.7:1 boundary.
Skeptical homeowner comparing builders: Google Guaranteed is a text line, no link to read reviews, "Fast service. Fair pricing." has nothing behind it, the service pages repeat the home page.
Returning blog reader: Blog is only in the mobile menu, posts are about 100 words, "More from the blog" shows two cards in a three-column grid.

## Minor Observations

Hero kicker is hard to read over the busy video; film watermark shows clipped on the discount band; trust strip's third item has no sub-label; mobile bar says "Call" with no number; header phone number is rendered but always hidden.

## Questions to Consider

If the 3D render is the differentiator, why is it not the page's centre of gravity? What does a patio-cover page say that a pool page does not? What if "vacation" were the visual idea rather than a tagline?
